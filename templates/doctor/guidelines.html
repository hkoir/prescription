1. Scope & Eligibility
  • Applies to all verified, registered doctors.
  • Maintain accurate profile & valid license documentation.
2. Booking & Notifications

You will receive booking notifications via:

You are responsible for checking notifications promptly so no patient is delayed.

3. Real-Time Consultation
  • Patients get a secure link to start real-time chat / WebRTC video call immediately after booking.
  • Stay online / open the website to receive chat/video calls; join the session from your dashboard.
4. Consultation Workflow
  • Review symptoms, images, short videos, and medical history.
  • Use the platform’s standard prescription template only.
  • Communicate professionally and document key clinical reasoning.
5. Clinical & Compliance Standards
  • Follow applicable laws, guidelines, and medical council rules.
  • Request additional tests/referrals when indicated.
  • Respect patient privacy; never export/share data outside the platform.
6. Fees & Payments
  • Consultation fees are added to your dashboard after prescription submission.
  • View earnings, pending/paid amounts, and prescription history in the dashboard.
  • Payouts available: {% for opt in payout_options %} {{ opt }} {% endfor %} — subject to platform fees, gateway charges, refunds/chargebacks.
7. Cancellations & No-Shows
  • Mark no-shows with proper status; fees may be partial/zero per policy.
  • If you can’t attend, notify and reschedule promptly. Repeated no-shows may lead to action.
8. Availability Expectation

Patients can book anytime. Please remain online as much as possible or configure clear availability windows.

9. Prohibited Conduct
  • Off-platform payments/solicitation; sharing personal contacts.
  • Inappropriate prescribing or controlled substances without proper clearance.
  • Harassment, discrimination, or unprofessional conduct.
10. Support & Escalation

Use in-app support for technical issues; report clinical safety concerns immediately.

11. Consultation Service Charge

When a doctor registers as a specialized doctor, they will have the option to set a standard consultation fee in our prescription template. For any patient requesting a chat or video call, the doctor may choose to apply this fee or provide the service free of charge if the patient has a valid booking. If the doctor decides to charge additionally for chat/video consultations, they must specify the amount. Keep in mind that higher fees may discourage patients from booking future appointments.

12. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
