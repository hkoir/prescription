Doctor Service Guidelines
  1. Availability: Doctors should remain online as much as possible to receive patient bookings and real-time WebRTC calls.
  2. Notifications: You will be notified of new patient bookings via Firebase push notifications, web notifications, and SMS. Check notifications regularly to avoid delays.
  3. Consultation: Patients will fill symptoms, medical history, and upload images/videos. You must review this before providing prescription.
  4. Real-time Chat/Video: A link will be provided to start instant chat or video call. Ensure you are online and in the chat room to accept calls.
  5. Prescription Template: You must use the system’s prescription template for consistency.
  6. Fees Tracking: Consultation fees (chat/video/in-person) will be recorded in your dashboard. Track your earnings, pending payments, and prescriptions given.
  7. Payment: Doctors receive daily/weekly/monthly payments. Keep your wallet/bank details updated in your profile.
  8. Professional Conduct: Always provide timely consultations. Avoid unnecessary delays for patients.
  9. Follow-ups: Follow-up consultations must respect validity days as defined in the system.
  10. Consultation Service Charge: You can define standard consultation fees. For chat/video calls, you may choose free or charged. Higher fees may reduce patient bookings.
